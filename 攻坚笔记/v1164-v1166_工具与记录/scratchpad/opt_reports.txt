**What I fixed:**
1. The end-of-round re-ask was paid as a full correction. It cleared the 错题本 (1 to 0), moved the review box from 0 to 1, and paid +2 纠错金. On the batch HEAD, one deliberate miss plus the re-ask paid +23 coins, against +1 for a right answer. A re-ask now counts as a normal right answer only.
2. 极速 60 秒: once a word had been re-asked, it scored 0 every time it came back for the rest of the round.
3. 听音辨义: a re-ask added one extra pet practice count.
4. 完形填空: the lesson card hid the sentence and read only the bare word. Before, a wrong answer read the whole example sentence.
5. 完形填空: when the words left in the pool cannot be shown (pig, eagle and cow on the animal island, whose example sentences use the plural), the round ended without re-asking the missed word. This also made the batch's own test fail on some runs.
6. Leaving the game while the card was showing: the card stayed on screen, and its timer then started the next question and read words aloud. In 打字大师 it also switched typing back on.
7. With 一键静音 on, tapping the card made the next word's auto-read audible.
8. A word could earn +15 毕业金 twice: once when its 错题本 count reached 0, and again when its review plan graduated in 今日复习.
9. 单词大陆③ and 星海 share the same words. After a word was cleared on 星海, opening 单词大陆③ brought it back as wrong, dropped its review box back to 0, and also corrupted the 星海 side. The 错题本 part of this already happens on origin/main; the review box part is new in this batch.

I also made three of the batches' tests stable. In each case the assertion is unchanged, and each still fails on origin/main.
