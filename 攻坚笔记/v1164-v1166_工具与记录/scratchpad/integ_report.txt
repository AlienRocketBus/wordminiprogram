What I changed:

1. Room data layer, reported by the lifecycle E2E. A guest could tap 返回 while the host was reading an old copy of the seat. The host then confirmed a guest who had already gone, played against nobody, and the room could never reopen. Now:
   - The host writes its confirmation and then waits for the guest to answer: `acked` (or `guestReady`) on the guest's own seat.
   - If the seat is missing on 3 reads, is marked left, or stays silent for 20 s, the host withdraws the confirmation: the room is `open` again with the next seat number (epoch +1).
   - The guest writes `acked` (3 tries) as soon as it sees the confirmation, again in markReady, and again in its heartbeat.
   - A tap on 取消 now wins over a confirmation that arrives at the same moment.
   - A guest whose confirmation was withdrawn notices after 3 fresh reads (`opponentGone` → `kicked`). This also closes the data agent's "dead guest gets confirmed, then host gets a free abandon win" risk.

2. Modes B/C ("两人都答完才进下一个词") now really wait for both players.
   - Data layer: claimRound/skipRound write `hostAns`/`hostPts` (host's room doc) or `guestAns`/`guestPts` (guest's seat), each player on their own doc only. roundState returns `oppAns` and `oppPts`.
   - UI: the new `nextWord()` waits for the opponent to finish the word, at most until this word's deadline + 3 s, with a live countdown line. The opponent's live score now includes their 60/80 second-place points and never goes down.
   - New E2E check: the two phones are never more than 1 word apart.

3. `wordsFromSeed` only picks letters-only words. 17 of the 712 words (ice cream, T-shirt, fire station…) cannot be typed on the PK keyboard. About 1 game in 5 used to contain one and it always timed out. Both phones use the same rule, so the word lists still match.

4. `sessionErr` now routes by the `kind` that auth returns, instead of guessing from the reason text. The session is cached for 20 s and dropped on account change or on expired/session errors. Before, every database read and write called ensureSession and wrote a trace line, filling the 60-line phone trace.

5. Auth:
   - loginByPassword retries the mail alias only when the username was not found.
   - `friendly()` turns credentials not found / unauthenticated / invalid_grant into 「登录没真正完成」 instead of 「没找到这个用户名」. This fixed the 1 failure in test_auth_cloud.
   - ensureProfile reads its new profile back up to 4 times, 500 ms apart, so a slow read no longer leaves the child without a cid.

6. The ladder home's leaderboard box shows the real problem by kind: config → 云端设置还差一步 with 技术信息; network, session and expired each get their own message. It no longer says 网络不太顺 for everything. A failed board push is now logged.
